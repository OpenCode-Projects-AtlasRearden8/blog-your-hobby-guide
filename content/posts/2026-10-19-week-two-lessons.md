Title: What Week Two Taught Me
Date: 2026-10-19

Weeks two and three of any hobby blog are where it gets interesting —
not because the writing is good, but because the workload is honest.
Here is what this one learned.

## Three small lessons

- The 10 + 20 + 10 shape from the manual works. Ten minutes of notes,
  twenty of drafting, ten of tidying. Three real sessions make one
  post, and none of them feel like homework.
- Publishing before perfecting feels wrong for exactly one week. Then
  it feels like freedom.
- The checker script catches more than expected. Two broken internal
  links this week — a typo'd filename and an image path. `check.py`
  found both before any reader could.

## One open question

Does a weekly rhythm need a fixed publishing day, or just a fixed
publishing *week*? Next week this will be decided, argued about, and
reported back — which, not coincidentally, is next week's post.
