Title: AI Helped Draft This Post (And Here's Exactly How)
Date: 2026-10-26

This post does what its title says: it is an example of the disclosure
Chapter 5 suggests, shown rather than told. It also demonstrates the
honest middle ground most bloggers will find themselves in.

## How the draft actually happened

1. Ten minutes of notes in `ideas.md` about what the week felt like.
2. The notes were handed to a modest local AI model with one sentence:
   "Turn these notes into a friendly first-draft, short paragraphs."
3. The draft came back 70% wrong in tone and 90% right in structure.
4. Every sentence was kept or replaced by the human whose handwriting
   you (did not) see, because HTML is the finished product and drafts
   are drafts.

## What was verified by hand

- The claim about `check.py` finding two broken links: checked against
  the actual version control history. True.
- That a 10-minute capture-file habit produces usable notes: verified
  by directly consulting the capture file.

## The plain disclosure, as it appears on the about page

Some posts here are drafted with AI assistance, then reviewed, edited,
and verified before publishing. Anything factual that is wrong is a
human error, and it gets fixed when spotted.

That's the whole trick. One paragraph, full trust preserved, and the
blank page never wins again.
