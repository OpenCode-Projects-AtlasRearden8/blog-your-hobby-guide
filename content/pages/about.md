Title: About This Blog

Two paragraphs is enough here. One: what the blog is about and why it
exists. Two: who is writing and how to reach them.

A note on AI, in plain English: some posts are drafted with AI
assistance, then reviewed, edited, and fact-checked before publishing.

when spotted.

There are no ads, no trackers, and no analytics. If you want to say
hello, use the reply-by-email link on any post's home page.
