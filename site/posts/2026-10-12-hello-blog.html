This is the first post of an example hobby blog. It exists so the blog has something to show on day one — and so you can see exactly what a plain, honest first post looks like.

What this blog is

One person writing about one hobby, once a week. No ads, no tracking, no fancy stack: Markdown files turned into HTML by a build script that fits inside one file.

Why the date in the filename

Both of these files are named YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md. That little convention keeps every post in order, in every folder view, forever — an archive for free.

What you might do next

If you are reading this as part of the manual in docs/, your next step is Chapter 3: copy content/posts/_template.md, rename it with today's date, and write fifty words about why you picked your hobby. Then run the build and look at your own site.
